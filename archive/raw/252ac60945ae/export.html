Jupyter User Survey

The Jupyter Foundation is excited to gather your feedback on the Jupyter user experience through a brief survey. If you had a magic wand, what would you change or add to Jupyter? Your insights are incredibly important as we continue to invest in making Jupyter better for everyone.

The survey takes only about 3 minutes to complete, and we highly encourage you to participate. The survey will close on Sunday, June 28th.
